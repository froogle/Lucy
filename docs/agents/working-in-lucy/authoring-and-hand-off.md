# Authoring and Hand-off

- **Authoring:** `Thinking::open_delegate` spawns a Delegate under Landlock (`plugins::sandbox::confine_delegate`). It runs `plugins::sandbox::delegate_program`: a Provider's `program` as given, else the CLI found on `PATH` past symlinks and a mise shim (`mise which <name>`, asked unconfined), and only that executable's folder is made readable, never mise's config or installs; `Thinking::executable` reports it for `provider.check`. A Delegate's stderr is piped and its last lines kept: a dead Delegate sends `ThinkingEvent::Died(Exit)` with its exit status and stderr tail, and an error `result` line (`is_error` or an `error_*` subtype) sends `Failed(message)` (a Session treats it as `TurnEnd`). A build that gives up logs why; the stderr tail goes only to the journal and a one-line form to the notification. `FakeDelegate::open_on` runs later Delegates on a real adapter (a fake `claude`) while the Session stays scripted. A Codex Delegate's thread runs with `danger-full-access`: lucyd's Landlock rules confine it, not Codex's sandbox, so it keeps the network to fetch dependencies. In the harness, `thinking.delegate` is the scripted Delegate: read its Turns, write the Plugin into `workspace().dir` with `stage_plugin`, and call the Delegate tools through its relay. On `submit_plugin` lucyd runs the staging dir's `build.sh` then `test.sh`, sandboxed, so a test scripts a pass or a failure there. Lucy's build lines and questions wait in the runner until the Session is quiet (`Command::TurnOrNextSession`, `runner::When`); `barrier` in `tests/authoring.rs` orders a test after them. A build outlives its Session: its question and Approval go through `runner::ask_when_quiet_or_next_session`, which keeps them in the runner for the next Session; "Is that the one?" is `When::Kept`, kept behind the answer's Turn without a notification of its own. A budget counts on `Adapters.clock` (see **Clock** in `harness.md`). With no Session open, or if it ends first, a build line is shown as its Build notification, then told once at the next Session start; its text is for the model, never shown. "cancel it" is matched locally in `session::Session`, never sent to the model. The template (`crates/lucyd/plugin-template/`) is built into lucyd; build it by hand after changing it.
- **Hand-off:** `hand_to_claude` starts a `hand_off::Job` through `Thinking::open_hand_off` with an `adapters::HandOff` (folder and whole environment). lucyd captures the environment with `$SHELL -lic 'env -0'`, both taken from `Options.plugin_env`, which is lucyd's own environment and the fallback; the 5 s wait counts on `Adapters.clock`. The `claude` adapter removes the billing variables from it (the guard), runs with `--setting-sources user,project,local`, the relay added beside the user's MCP servers and `--permission-prompt-tool mcp__lucy__permission`, and keeps the transcript. Its `result` line arrives as `ThinkingEvent::Final`, and the conversation id as `ThinkingEvent::Conversation`. The permission tool asks through `runner::ask_at` with `When::QuietOrNextSession` ("Claude wants to {description}. Okay?", the input's `description` or else the tool's name, with the tool name and input in the Details window, `hand_off::permission_items`, or a `Details` notification when the window can't be shown) and answers `allow` with `updatedInput` on a yes, `deny` otherwise. Its `ask_user` and `ask_typed` (`input::ask_typed_at`) block the call until answered and go through `When::QuietOrNextSession`, so they wait until the Session is quiet; the answer is the tool's result, never a new Turn, since a Hand-off ends at its Turn's end. `report_progress` is a `HandOff` notification only. A Hand-off outlives its Session: with no Session open, its questions and permission requests are notified at once (`HandOff` notifications) and asked at the next Session, and its result is told then. Its outcome is shown once as a notification; `Command::ShownTurnOrNextSession` keeps the Turn without another unless a Session ends before telling it. `HandOffs::working` drives the `working` flag in `state`. One runs at a time: a second `hand_to_claude` is refused with the queue offer for the model to ask, and `queue: true` queues it (a queue of one, started when the running one ends, dropped by a cancel). Its budget (`[handoff] budget_mins`) is one timer on `Adapters.clock`, set when the Job starts and never paused, Session or not; at the budget, or on a cancel, the Job drops its model, which stops `claude`. "cancel it" in `session::Session` stops whichever of a build and a Hand-off runs; with both, `HandOffs::ask_which_to_cancel` asks through `runner::ask` and matches the answer locally (`hand_off::which_to_cancel`), stopping only the Hand-off it asked about. A cancel that drops the queued request says so. Every ending saves its conversation id and folder to `hand-off.toml` in the state dir (`Paths.state`) before notifying, or deletes the file when it ended without one, so the record is always the latest Hand-off's; `continue: true` reads it when its Job starts (so a queued one carries on the Hand-off before it) and sets `adapters::HandOff.resume`, which the `claude` adapter passes as `--resume`; a record that can't be read is logged (never its contents) and the model is told Lucy couldn't read it. In the harness, `Harness::restart` stops lucyd and starts another on the same dirs with new fakes; `thinking.hand_off` is the scripted Hand-off (`hand_off()`, `answer`); its `open_on` runs it on a real adapter, with `Harness::with_env` as lucyd's environment (the fake `claude` records `./argv` and `./environ` in the folder).
